LESSON 1 · 12 MIN

TODAY'S TOPIC

First hellos in Korean

👩‍🏫

Hi Naomi! In this 12-minute lesson, you’ll learn one greeting you can use almost anywhere, how to say your name, and the polite ending that makes you sound friendly and respectful. First we’ll understand it, then say it, hear it, and read it—just like in a real class.

By the end, you can:✓ greet someone politely✓ say “My name is Naomi”✓ recognize the polite sound 요 yo

CLASS PLAN · 12 MIN

  1. Warm-up · 2 min 01
  2. Words · 3 min 02
  3. Say it · 3 min 03
  4. Hear & read · 3 min 04
  5. Culture · 1 min 05
Culture note

Korean has different speech levels. The ending 요 yo keeps your sentence polite and warm—great for teachers, adults, and new people.

HANGUL LAB · 4 MIN

WRITE FROM THE BEGINNING

Meet Hangul, letter by letter.

Hangul is Korea’s alphabet. It was designed so ordinary people could learn to read and write—so you can too. We’ll learn one letter at a time, practise its sound, then use it in real Korean words.

LETTER 1 OF 5

YOUR WRITING ROUTINE

  1. Look at the letter
  2. Listen to its sound
  3. Trace it in the air
  4. Write it three times
  5. Find it in a word
Teacher tip

Writing a new alphabet can feel strange at first. Go slowly; neat strokes matter less than noticing the shape and sound.
